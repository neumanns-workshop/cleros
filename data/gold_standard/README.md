# Cleros: Ancient Greek Religious Corpus

## Overview

Digital collection of Orphic religious texts with parallel Greek-English translations and scholarly annotations.

## Corpus Contents

### Orphic Argonautica
- **Genre**: Epic narrative poetry
- **Period**: 4th-6th century CE
- **Content**: Jason's heroic quest with Orphic theological elements
- **Lines**: 1,376 lines of dactylic hexameter

### Orphic Lithica
- **Genre**: Didactic poetry on mineralogy
- **Period**: 4th-6th century CE
- **Content**: Magical and medicinal properties of stones
- **Lines**: 779 lines of hexameter verse

### Orphic Hymns
- **Genre**: Ritual religious poetry
- **Period**: 2nd-3rd century CE
- **Content**: 88 hymns to deities + proem + appendix
- **Lines**: 1,173 total lines

## Available Formats

Each collection is available in four formats:

1. **Scholarly JSON** (`*.json`) -- Complete metadata, philological apparatus, textual notes
2. **Parallel Edition** (`*_parallel.json`) -- Side-by-side Greek and English
3. **Greek Text** (`*_greek.txt`) -- Pure ancient Greek text with line numbers
4. **English Translation** (`*_english.txt`) -- Complete English translations

## Source Texts

- **Orphic Hymns**: Greek text from Thomas Taylor, *The Mystical Hymns of Orpheus* (London, 1787)
- **Orphic Argonautica**: Greek text from Hermann Abel, *Orphica* (Leipzig: Teubner, 1885)
- **Orphic Lithica**: Greek text from Hermann Abel, *Orphica* (Leipzig: Teubner, 1885)

English translations generated by GPT-5 reasoning system from original Greek texts.
